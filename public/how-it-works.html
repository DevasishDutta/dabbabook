01

Login

Open the dashboard and sign in with your credentials. Each tiffin business gets their own private login.

02

Dashboard Overview

Instantly see Total Orders, Active Orders, Pending Payments, and Today's Meals — all in one place. Recent orders appear below.

03

All Orders

See every order with customer details, plan type, session, and payment status. Filter by Active, New, Pending, or Completed.

04

Generate Kitchen List

Pick a date and session (Lunch / Dinner / All). Hit Generate. Your kitchen list is ready instantly — Veg and Non-Veg colour coded.

05

Skip Orders

Customer on leave? Hit Skip on any order. It gets crossed out and excluded from the count. Hit Undo to bring it back.

06

Download Kitchen List

One click exports the kitchen list as a CSV file — opens directly in Excel or Google Sheets. Skipped orders are excluded automatically.

07

Meals Remaining

Every order shows how many meals are left — colour coded Green (plenty), Amber (running low), Red (ending soon). Never lose track.

08

Separate Veg & Non-Veg Lists

Filter by Veg Only or Non-Veg Only before generating. The kitchen gets two separate colour-coded lists — zero confusion.
